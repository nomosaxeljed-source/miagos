POPULAR HIKES AND TRAILS

Miagao offers a variety of hiking trails and natural adventures for visitors. Here are some highlights:

  • Tinagong Dagat (Hidden Sea): A hidden lake in Barangay Ongyod, elevated approximately 3,000 feet above sea level, accessible via a challenging hike.
  • Bato Sampaw (Big Rock): A large rock formation in Barangay Onop.
  • Tigmalapad Peak: An easy hiking destination offering panoramis views of the mountains and teracces of Miagao.
  • Bugsukan Falls: A waterfall in Barangay Dalije, just a walking distance from the drop off site, perfect fopr swimming.
  • Eriquican Falls:A pictresque waterfall in Barangay Alimodias, located approximately 1 hour from the drop-off point.

    These trails provide a mix of adventure and natural beauty, making Miagao a great destination for outdoor enthusiasts.
