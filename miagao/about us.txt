<p>
Miagao is a coastal town with a hilly to mountainous terrain. Most of the flat areas in the municipality are found either in coasts or along the town's major rivers which include the Naulid, Tumagbok, Oyaoy, Bacauan, Tabunacan, Oyungan, Lanutan, and San Rafael rivers. A mountain range forms a natural boundary between Miagao and the province of Antique to the north.

The highest point in the municipality is the summit of Mount Tulajon, locally spelled as Tulahong, at 1,100 meters asl located in Barangay Ongyod.[</p>

<h3>Why People Love to Hikes and Trails</h3>

<ul>
<li>Physical Health: Hiking is a great way to burn calories, improve cardiovascular fitness, and build muscle strength. It also helps with weight management and can reduce stress levels.</li>
<li>Mental Clarity: The act of walking outdoors can help reduce cortisol levels, which are the stress hormones, and increase endorphin levels, which are mood-boosting chemicals.</li>
<li>Connection with Nature: Hiking allows individuals to immerse themselves in natural landscapes, which can have a calming effect on the mind and a revitalizing impact on the body.</li>
<li>Social Interaction: Trails often provide opportunities for social interaction, whether it's hiking with friends or meeting new people. </li>
<li>Adventure and Exploration: Hiking can be an adventure, offering new experiences and challenges as hikers explore different terrains and environments. 
</li>
</ul>

<h3>Basic Needs for Hikes and Trails</h3>

<ul>
<li>Clothing: Moisture-wicking shirts, layers for changing conditions, and appropriate footwear. </li>
<li>First Aid Kit: Essential for emergencies, including pain relievers, water filter, and a multi-tool.</li>
<li>Water: Bring enough water for your hike, especially for longer distances.</li>
<li>Food: Pack snacks and a water bottle to stay hydrated.</li>
<li>Navigation Tools: A map, compass, and a whistle are essential for safe navigation. </li>
</ul>
